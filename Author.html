Author's Challenge

A conjecture states that if:

$$\frac{\underbrace{dd\cdots dd}_{n\text{ times}}}{\underbrace{d+d+\cdots +d+d}_{n\text{ times}}}\in \mathbb{N}$$

If d is a digit of the number dd...dd such that:

$$\left(0<d\leq 9\right),\left(n\in\mathbb{N}\right)$$

Then the only solutions of n are in the form:

$$\left(n=37^a\cdot3^b\right)\forall \left(a,b\in\mathbb{W}\right)$$

Prove this conjecture true or false.
