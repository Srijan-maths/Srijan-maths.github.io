100 of the Toughest Problems for Advanced Students

(Original Problems from Class 9 to 12)

This book contains one hundred original mathematics problems designed to challenge advanced students. The problems cover a wide range of topics including algebra, geometry, number theory, calculus, inequalities, combinatorics and other areas of elementary mathematics. Every problem has been created with an emphasis on originality, mathematical depth and elegant reasoning.

Author's Challenge Problems

Selected problems are designated as Author's Challenge. These problems are eligible for the Hall of Fame. Readers are encouraged to attempt these problems independently and submit complete proofs before consulting any official solution. The first accepted solver of each Author's Challenge problem will be recognised in the Hall of Fame.

100 of the Toughest Problems for Advanced Students — Solutions

This companion volume contains complete, detailed solutions to the problems presented in the main book. Each solution includes rigorous proofs together with the intended mathematical ideas behind the problem.

Important: Solutions to all Author's Challenge problems are intentionally omitted from this book. These official solutions are released exclusively through the Srijan Maths website after the corresponding Hall of Fame challenge has been completed and officially closed.

How the Two Books Work Together

  • The main book contains all one hundred original problems.
  • Some problems are marked as Author's Challenge.
  • The companion solution book provides complete solutions to all non-challenge problems.
  • Solutions to Author's Challenge problems are published only on the official Srijan Maths website after the challenge has closed.
  • Readers who solve an Author's Challenge before the official solution is released may earn a place in the Hall of Fame.
