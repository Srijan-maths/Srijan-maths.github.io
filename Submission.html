General Submission Rules

To submit a solution, send an email to the author with a clear photograph or scanned copy of your work attached. Please include the problem number in the subject line of your email.

A submission will be considered only if it contains a complete, step-by-step derivation together with a rigorous mathematical proof. Partial solutions, unsupported answers, or incomplete arguments will not be considered.

The author's decision regarding the correctness, completeness, and acceptance of any submission shall be final.

If a submission is accepted, the author will contact the solver by email.

Hall of Fame Rules

Problems designated as Author's Challenge are eligible for the Hall of Fame.

  • Only the first solver whose complete and correct solution is accepted by the author will be inducted into the Hall of Fame.
  • Once a problem has been solved, verified, and officially closed, no further Hall of Fame submissions for that problem will be accepted.
  • With the solver's permission, their name will be published on the official Srijan Maths Hall of Fame.
