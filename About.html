About Srijan Maths

Srijan Maths is an independent mathematical project dedicated to the creation of original, thought-provoking problems for advanced students. Rather than emphasising routine computation, the project focuses on problems that reward creativity, rigorous reasoning, and elegant proof. The collection spans topics including algebra, geometry, number theory, calculus, combinatorics, inequalities, and other areas of elementary mathematics.

About the Author

My name is Srijan Chatterjee, and I am a student from India with a strong interest in mathematics, problem solving, computer science, and electronics. I began creating original mathematical problems out of curiosity and a desire to explore ideas beyond standard textbook exercises. Over time, this developed into a larger project aimed at producing challenging problems that encourage deep mathematical thinking.

Every problem published under Srijan Maths is designed to be original and carefully constructed, with an emphasis on mathematical elegance rather than unnecessary complexity.

Mission

The objective of Srijan Maths is to inspire students to think beyond memorised techniques and develop genuine problem-solving ability. Mathematics is at its best when it encourages curiosity, logical reasoning, and persistence.

Acknowledgements
