About the Hall of Fame

The Hall of Fame honours individuals who have submitted exceptional solutions to official Srijan Maths problems. Recognition may be awarded for:

  • First correct solution.
  • Most elegant proof.
  • Outstanding mathematical creativity.
  • Consistent excellence across multiple problems.

Only verified submissions are eligible.
