About

Welcome to Srijan Maths by Srijan Chatterjee.

This project aims to publish original mathematics problems covering algebra, geometry, number theory, calculus, inequalities, functional equations, combinatorics and many other topics.

The collection is intended for students preparing for Olympiads, JEE, and anyone who enjoys solving difficult mathematics.

Combinatorics

Miscellaneous

Anything that refuses to fit neatly into a category. "Mathematics enjoys chaos; to develop your thinking you must not get adapted to one type."
